# DSH SheetFlow v0.1.0-pre

Fill recurring Excel templates, review reconciliation exceptions, and trace every value to its source.

**AI suggests. You confirm. SheetFlow executes.**

In one fresh synthetic blind test, `deepseek-v4-pro` with `max` reasoning took **34.66 seconds** to inspect the tables and propose a mapping. It selected the correct business columns and template region, but placed both source headers one row too early. SheetFlow rejected the recipe with **`header-changed` before writing to the template**. The validation operator corrected the header rows **2 → 3** and **3 → 4**, reviewed the exceptions, and exported a new copy while leaving all three inputs unchanged.

The mistake and correction are part of the evidence: a proposed AI mapping can be wrong, a deterministic guard can stop it, and a reviewed correction can complete the workflow. Review and correction in this test were performed by Codex as the validation operator, rather than signed off by a human user.

The resulting eight records contain **four matches and four retained exceptions**. Expected total: **2,794.40 CNY**. Received total: **2,529.51 CNY**. Difference: **−264.89 CNY**. Acknowledging an exception records review; it does not resolve the discrepancy or approve a payment.

## Preview limits

This is a **Pre-release**, not a production financial-delivery tool, accounting system or source of accounting, tax or financial advice. Independently verify AI mappings, amounts, formulas and exceptions before business use.

All example data is synthetic. **Only one real model turn** has been tested. Other models, real accounting datasets and complex workbooks remain unverified. The row-number inspection and prompt improvement was regression-tested locally but has not received another paid model test. Macros, protected target sheets, external workbook links and digitally signed workbooks are outside this preview.
